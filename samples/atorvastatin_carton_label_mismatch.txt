Complaint Source: Commercial Wholesaler & Distributor
Customer Name: Medix Central Healthcare Logistics Corp.
Facility Location: Chicago Distribution Center #4

2. PRODUCT & BATCH IDENTIFICATION
Product Name: Atorvastatin Calcium 20mg Film-Coated Tablets
Product Strength / Grade: 20mg FDF Tablet
Batch / Lot Number: ATV-FDF-4402
Manufacturing Date: 2024-05-18
Expiry Date: 2027-05-17
Quantity Affected: 450 units (Secondary Cartons)
Originating Site Block: Secondary Packaging Block D
Impacted Non-Product Materials (NPM): Outer Unit Folding Box & Printed Barcode Label

3. COMPLAINT DETAILS
Complaint Type: Packaging & Labeling Defect
Complaint Date: 2024-09-01
Detailed Complaint Description:
During inbound barcode scanning and optical character verification (OCV), our automated sorting line flagged 450 cartons of Atorvastatin 20mg Lot ATV-FDF-4402. The thermal inkjet printed lot number and expiration date are severely smudged, with the last two digits of the expiry year illegible on 38% of units examined. The internal blister packs have correct stamping; defect is isolated to the secondary carton printing ribbon tension.

4. INITIAL ASSESSMENT & PRIORITY
Initial Severity: Minor
Priority: Medium
Clinical Risk: Low patient risk since blister foil data is intact; regulatory labeling non-conformance under GMP Chapter 5.
