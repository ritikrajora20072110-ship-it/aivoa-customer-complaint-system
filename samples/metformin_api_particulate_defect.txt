COMPLAINT & DEFECT DETAILS:
- Complaint Type: Critical Contamination / Foreign Particulate Defect
- Complaint Date: 2024-08-19
- Detailed Description:
Upon sampling and 100% sift testing of Drum #2 and Drum #3 of Batch MET-API-9921 prior to granulation charging, QC analysts identified multiple visible black metallic-like particulate contaminants (>0.5 mm) embedded within the crystalline powder. Microscopic FTIR analysis confirmed the particulate to be 316L stainless steel wear debris, likely originating from a centrifugal mill or drying agitation blade during API micronization.

INITIAL ASSESSMENT & RISK PRIORITY:
- Initial Severity: Critical
- Priority: Urgent
- Patient Safety Impact: Potential injection/tablet contamination risk; complete batch quarantine mandated under 21 CFR Part 211.84.
